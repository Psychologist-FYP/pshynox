The 2,416 accepted grades are not represented as 2,416 individually inspected labels.

## Audit before threshold selection

The Codex session checked all ten no-answer questions against the complete dialogues,
all 246 ambiguous pairs, and a seed-20261103 stratified sample of 89 unflagged grades,
including zeros (up to three per grade per conversation where available). Findings
are an **AI consistency check**, not independent human review.

All ten absent-answer candidates remain supported as absent in the supplied text.
There are 68 proposed corrections. Most remove grade 1 from elicitation prompts,
empty acknowledgments or background outside the defined question. Some narrow
formerly direct labels to contextual evidence; one explicitly contextualized
four-cup confirmation becomes grade 2. Every correction has a rationale based on
query scope and dialogue, not retrieval scores. Original accepted labels were not
overwritten. The experiment snapshots apply these proposals as pending AI labels;
human acceptance is still required before treating them as independently reviewed.

In the annotation UI, select an accepted AI query and a flagged turn. An **AI
consistency-check proposal** appears when a correction is suggested. Click **Load
proposal for my review**, inspect the context, and save explicitly. Prior judgments
and AI references are retained in review history when a human edits a grade.
Machine-readable findings are in `review-experiments/audit-results.json`.

## Measurements and focused questions

Existing Recall@5/10, Precision@5, MRR@10, nDCG@10 and no-answer metrics remain.
New metrics are **Grade-2 hit@5**, **Grade-2 recall@5**, and **Grade-2 recall@10**.
Their denominator includes only questions with at least one grade-2 turn. Questions
with only grade-1 context are excluded from direct-evidence averages, rather than
counted as direct-answer failures. No-answer queries remain separate.

Seven explicitly graded focused development questions accompany the 28 baseline
questions. They isolate daily smoking amount, exertional symptoms, class attendance,
school suspension, physical-exam timing, the meth-use interval and the proposed
coffee cup to drop. The meth-interval question is a matched narrow control; the
baseline set itself mixes broad and narrow questions, so this is descriptive,
not a balanced causal comparison of query formulations. Group metrics are saved
separately in `focused-comparison.json`; groups are not silently pooled for claims
that focused questions always improve retrieval. No new final-set wording or
labels were generated by AI.

## Method-specific thresholds

One completed development retrieval run covers the 35 baseline/focused questions.
The threshold sweep filters saved, ordered top-ten scores using `>=`, which is
exact for this ranker’s scalar cutoff. It does not rerun model inference at every
cutoff. Each method has its own candidate score grid and frozen threshold.

The initial equal-weight objective could select near-total abstention for lexical
methods. Those unconstrained curves/configurations are retained. Development
inspection led to revision 2: preserve at least 80% of baseline direct-evidence
hit@5, then maximize the equal-weight average of direct-hit@5 and no-answer
abstention; break ties by direct-hit and then direct-recall@10. This revision was
based on development failures before inspecting the held-out metrics.

The selected settings are **None for BM25**, **None for TF-IDF**, **0.7856051921844482
for E5**, and **0.03125763125763126 for hybrid**. None means retain baseline behavior.
These are exploratory candidates from a very small, AI-labelled development set.
They are frozen in `review-experiments/frozen-method-config.json`, bound to the
ranker hash, model revision, audit hash and development snapshot. They have not
been installed as application defaults or tuned on test results.

E5 and hybrid improve development abstention but their test-pilot no-answer false
return rates remain 100%. Hybrid loses substantial direct-answer coverage on test.
A score cutoff alone does not establish answerability: high-scoring text about blood
pressure before cutting coffee cannot answer a question about measured changes
afterward. Test failures are reported, not used to change labels or retune cutoffs.
See `threshold-failures.json`, `threshold-curves.json` and `SUMMARY.md` for details.

## Independent human final set — awaiting annotation

After freezing configuration, we selected **annomi-101 (64 turns), annomi-79 (11),
and annomi-123 (27)** from unused test source groups. None had research queries or
exploration recorded, and their source groups do not overlap the pilot or each
other. Participant identities remain unavailable, so speaker independence cannot
be established. These conversations have not been AI-annotated or ranked.

In **Evaluation results**, click **Open independent blind final annotation** to open the separate form. You can also open `data/research/review-experiments/independent-final/review.html` directly in a browser.
It is a standalone blind annotation form, with no AI questions, labels or rankings.
Enter your reviewer name, read each complete conversation, and write at least three
realistic questions per conversation with intended evidence and exclusions. Include
an absent-answer question where appropriate. Grade every turn with a rationale.
Use **Download progress** for backups and **Restore progress** to move devices.
The form also persists local browser progress across reloads. Changing wording
clears that question’s grades. Export is blocked until required labels and any
full-conversation no-answer confirmations are complete.

This is a prepared packet, **not yet an independently human-labelled dataset**.
A human must supply its questions and labels without consulting AI or rankings.
Have another human check a subset and resolve disagreements before reporting.
After exporting `independent-final-labelled.json`, run once from the repository:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m services.retrieval.run_final \
  --labels /path/to/independent-final-labelled.json
```

The runner verifies frozen configuration, unchanged transcripts, stable IDs,
complete human labels and no-answer confirmations, then runs all four methods with
their frozen cutoffs. It refuses to overwrite an existing final result. Until human
labels are supplied, the independent evaluation remains pending. Do not tune settings
or labels to improve those final scores.
